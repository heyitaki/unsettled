What changed

  • No tab bar, no drawer. One scrolling document, the way the app already works in portrait. The board takes the full width of the pane, in a square frame when it is wider than it is tall and a taller one when it is not, scaled up until only a thin margin of sea is left. The sections below extend the page rather than scrolling inside themselves.
  • The map title is the library. Import, saved maps, and your other open boards all live behind it. That also gives phones back the board switcher, which the current build hides outright.
  • Import comes first on that screen. It is the reason you opened it, so it sits at the top as the one filled button. Import and export JSON, which you touch once a month, moved into the dots at the top left. Saved maps sort newest first and the label cycles through oldest and A–Z.
  • The dots are the roster. Claiming yourself is one tap from wherever you are, because the header stays put while the page moves under it.
  • An empty board says so. With no tiles on it there is nothing to rank, so the block below stops offering a ranking and offers the two ways out instead: import a screenshot, or place the tiles by hand.
  • A roster row does three things. Tap it to claim that seat, tap the name to rename in place, press and hold to drag it to a new seat. The claim chips are gone: four rows each carrying the same button repeated what the label above them already said, and it left nothing to tell the claimed row apart but a border.
  • Every map gets a colour. The hex beside a board name is derived from the name itself, so the same board wears the same colour in the header and in both lists, and two open boards are told apart before you read them.
  • Board tools live behind a pencil. Parked at the foot of the page, nothing told you they were there. The pencil in the header now swaps the analysis block for build mode, with Done and Cancel to leave. Terrain, numbers and structures each get one sideways-scrolling row.

Try it

  • Scroll the phone. The board leaves with the page; only the title, the dots and the options button stay. The options button holds undo and redo as a pair, plus randomize, export and clear.
  • Tap a pick. It draws your spot in your own colour and the planned follow-up faded behind it, and pulls the board back into view if you have scrolled past it.
  • Open the layout dropdown under the board and switch to 5–6 player. The board really is replaced: empty, with its own eleven harbours, in a frame taller than it is wide. Below it the ranking gives way to the two ways to fill it.
  • Tap the pencil. Build mode takes over the block below the board; everything above it stays put, so nothing jumps.
  • Hit Just imported: nobody is claimed yet, so the analysis block asks for that first instead of showing an empty list.
  • On the Maps screen, tap Newest under Saved maps to cycle the sort, and the dots in the corner for the JSON files.
  • On the Players screen, drag a row by its grip or press and hold anywhere on it. The snake draft below and the strip above the board both follow the new seat order. Tap a name to rename it; Enter keeps it, Escape puts it back.

The factor labels are renamed

The score pills read Production, Scarcity, Balance, Port and Robber, one word each. Balance stands in for "Diversity+recipes+numbers", which packed three ideas into a label too long to scan; the sixth factor, "Starting cards", would become Hand. That is a change to displayedFactors in AnalysisPanel.tsx, not just to this page.

Seat order is draft order

Dragging a row here does not just tidy a list: the snake is derived from it, so the line that reads "picking 3 and 6 of 8" changes with the drag. That means the roster order has to be the real source of the draft in the app, and a reorder has to re-run the analysis rather than only repaint the roster.

No timestamp on open boards

Saved maps show when they were last touched because that is how you decide whether to open or delete one. Open boards are the two or three you have open right now, all equally current, so the same column would say nothing. Leaving it off is also what keeps the two lists visibly different jobs rather than one list with two icons.

Cancel needs a snapshot

Board edits commit straight away today, with undo as the way back. For Cancel to mean anything, entering build mode has to snapshot the board and leaving by Cancel has to restore it. Small, but it is real work rather than a label.

The one cost

Tapping a low-ranked pick moves markers on a board that is off screen, so the page scrolls back up to show you. It is the honest trade for having no drawer, and it only bites on picks four and five.
