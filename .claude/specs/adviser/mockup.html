Settled

  1. One active player. Tapping a circle makes it that player's turn: the advice is theirs and the six actions record for them. End turn moves the ring to the next seat.
  2. No dot under a circle, here or in the draft ribbon. The ring is the only mark; who you are shows in the table and the roster.
  3. Straight arrows for undo and redo.
  4. The table sits under the turn order in both trees, with your own dev cards broken out by type beneath it; a card bought this turn is greyed until it can be played.
  5. Apply is on every recommendation. It commits when the advice is the whole fact, and otherwise opens the matching action filled in.
  6. Trade advice covers one shape, one or two cards of one resource for one card of another. Other trades can be recorded but get no verdict.
