## Decisions

- **Toggle.** The Ledger heading gains a `History` button on its right (after the award dots button when that shows): `HistoryGlyph` (a clock, 17px, line art on the 20-unit grid) plus the word `History`, Inter 600 12px, 30px tall, muted text with a `--glyph` icon, 1px transparent border, 6px radius. Hover turns text and icon `--accent-dark` with no background. Pressed (`aria-pressed="true"`): `--accent-dark` text and icon, 1px `--accent` border, 7% accent wash. It shows only once the game has a recorded turn (`game.turn` defined).
- **Swap.** While History is on, `Record your turn` and Best moves are not rendered; a `History` panel takes their place below the Ledger with the usual 12px gap (`.right-rail .best-moves-panel + .best-moves-panel`), on both trees. Entering build mode turns History off. History on/off and the viewed turn are temporary workspace UI state like `viewedPick`: not persisted, not in undo. The viewed turn resets to now on any commit, undo, redo or tab switch.
- **History panel.** Heading `History` with a muted `16 turns` count on the right (12px 600). The list has no hairline under the heading. Rows newest first: the turn in progress first (`Now`), then every finished turn, then `Game start`. Round labels go before the first row of each round, `Round r` with r = ceil(turn / seats), and `Setup` before `Game start`: 11px 600 muted, left-aligned with the heading, no rule after them, 10px above and 2px below (8px above the first). Rows have a hairline between them except right after a round label. Row: 12px left and 10px right padding, 10px vertical; grid `20px 1fr auto`, 10px gap. Turn disc: 20px circle in the player's colour, the turn number in Inter 800 11px tabular, `readableInk` text; a white player's disc gets a 1.5px ink border. `Game start` has a tint disc with a `--line` border and a small hex outline glyph. Title: name Inter 700 13px/1.3, and `Now` (Inter 600 11px, `--accent-dark`) after the name on the turn in progress. Summary under it: muted 11px/1.4, one line with ellipsis (`turnSummary`). Right column: the roll in Inter 800 15px tabular ink with token pip dots under it (3px dots, 1.5px apart, `--glyph`, count as on a number token); a 7 is `--accent` with a small `RobberGlyph` (7 by 9px) instead of pips; nothing before the roll.
- **Selection.** Hover changes nothing. The selected row has only a 3px ink bar on its left (`box-shadow: inset 3px 0 0 var(--ink)`) and opens: the summary is replaced by the details, and the gap under the name is 2px (`padding-bottom: 2px` on the row button). Details, indented to the name (42px left, 10px right, 11px bottom, 4px gaps): the production line (the `move-prod` markup the Rolled step uses; `Nobody produces` muted when a non-7 roll produced nothing; nothing on a 7 or before the roll) then the log as `move-log` tick lines in ink 12px. Default selection when History opens is Now. ArrowUp/ArrowDown inside the panel step to the newer/older turn.
- **Height and scroll.** Desktop: while History is on the right rail stretches to the row height (`align-self: stretch`, flex column) and the History panel takes the rest (`flex: 1 1 0; min-height: 280px`), its list scrolling inside; the list runs to the panel's side and bottom borders (negative margins, padding back inside, 6px bottom padding). Phone, and a desktop window of 980px or less where the rail sits under the board: the list is capped at 440px and scrolls inside. Selecting a row scrolls it into view inside the list only. Once the list is scrolled, the heading shows a faint 1px rule (7% ink) and a 12px shadow below it (5.5% ink fading through 3% and 1.2% to 0), spanning the panel's full width; no fade on the rows.
- **Viewing a past turn.** Selecting a row other than Now shows the board and the Ledger as they were at the end of that turn (`Game start`: when the first turn began). The Ledger heading adds `After turn 6` (`At game start`) in Inter 600 12px `--accent-dark` after `Ledger`; its active bar marks that turn's player (no row at `Game start`). Board and Ledger are read-only while a past turn shows: board clicks and hovers do nothing (pan, zoom and Reset still work), Ledger rows do not open, the award menu is hidden. Terrain, numbers and ports always come from the live board.
- **Board controls.** While History is on (outside build mode) a bar sits centred 8px below the board on both trees: `‹` button, label, `›` button, a 1px by 16px `--line` divider, `Back to now`. Pill: 32px tall, 1px `--line` border, surface background, no shadow, Inter 600 12.5px (12px on the phone; the muted part 500). Label: a 10px swatch with a 1.5px ink border, `Turn 4` in ink, then muted `· White rolled 7` (`· White` before the roll); `Game start` alone at index 0. Arrow buttons 26px round, `--glyph`, hover `--accent-dark` on tint, disabled `--faint`. `Back to now` 12px `--accent-dark`, disabled `--faint`. Earlier is disabled at Game start; later and `Back to now` are disabled at Now. On desktop the board's max-height shrinks by 52px while History is on so the bar fits. On the phone, selecting a row reveals the board (`revealBoardIfScrolledPast`).

## Data

- `TurnState` gains `log?: string[]` (the turn's recorded action lines, oldest first) and `gains?` (what the roll produced, nonzero cards only, absent on a 7 and when nothing was produced). The Record panel's action log reads `turn.log`, so it now survives a reload. Recording an action appends with `appendLog`. The log's Undo button shows while the newest undo entry has the same turn player and one log line fewer.
- `Game.history?: { start: Position; turns: TurnRecord[] }`. `Position` is what play changes: buildings, roads, robber, stats, awards, devHand. `start` is taken when the first turn begins (`setActivePlayer` on a game with no `turn`), or at the first `End turn` of a game saved before this feature; until then such a game has no `Game start` row and Earlier is disabled at Now. `endTurn` appends a `TurnRecord`: player, roll, gains, log, the full robber, stats, awards and devHand, and the pieces as a diff against the previous record (`buildings`/`roads` keyed by id, `null` for a removed piece) so a long game stays small in storage. `historyGame(game, index)` folds the diffs: index 0 is `start`, index k the end of turn k.
- `newGame` (the clears, randomize, the layout switch) has no history, so a reset drops it. Undo restores whole games, so it undoes history too. A roster change keeps history; a removed player's rows read `Removed player` with a grey disc.
- `parseGame` accepts `history` and the new turn fields. An invalid `history` is dropped with the game still loading; it never makes a save corrupt.
