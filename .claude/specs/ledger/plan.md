# Ledger

The Best moves `Table` (Cards/Points switch, `Correct X by hand` box, `Your dev cards` line) leaves `Record your turn` and becomes its own `Ledger` section above it, in both trees from one component. Approved mock-up: https://claude.ai/artifact/2Vc3BBmjv5dRAKzbnPGki7 (option `Centred`, owner 2026-11-12). The mock sources are under `.claude/mock/ledger/` (git-excluded).

## Decisions

- **Placement.** `BestMovesPanel` renders the Ledger as the first section of its fragment, before `Record your turn`, with the same `className` plus `best-moves-panel ledger-panel`, so the desktop rail and the phone page stack it like the other two sections. It shows exactly when the table showed (a player is active). Heading `Ledger`, no buttons.
- **Header row**: empty, `Hand`, `Cards`, `VP`, muted 11px weight 600. `Cards` and `VP` are centred over their columns.
- **Rows.** One per player in seat order, separated by hairlines. The whole row is a button (`aria-expanded`, `aria-label="Correct <name>"`) that toggles that player's editor; one row open at a time. The active player's row (whose turn it is) has a 3px ink bar on its left edge (`box-shadow: inset 3px 0 0 var(--ink)`); an open row has the tint background. Grid columns `118px 1fr 40px 40px` with an 8px column gap; on the phone `100px 1fr 36px 36px` with a 6px gap. Min height 40px, padding 6px 8px.
- **Vertical centring.** Every cell is centred in the row (`align-items: center; align-content: center`) and the name, hand, Cards and VP cells share one 20px line height, which centres Inter's cap height in the box. Never baseline-align these cells: the name cell starts with the swatch, so its baseline is the swatch's bottom edge and the name floats. The owner checks this to the pixel.
- **Name cell**: 12px swatch circle (1.5px ink border), the name (13px weight 600, ellipsis), the `YOU` chip on the claimed player (`.you-chip`, kept at its own line height), then one red medal per award held: the road `StructureGlyph` for Longest road and the knight `CounterGlyph` for Largest army, both 14px, filled with `--accent`, titled `Longest road, +2 VP` / `Largest army, +2 VP`. Holders come from `computeStandings` (`hasLongestRoad`, `hasLargestArmy`), which already applies `Game.awards` and the incumbent-keeps-a-tie rule.
- **Hand cell** (12.5px weight 600, tabular figures, wraps with 3px/9px gaps): each resource the player holds as `ResourceGlyph` + count (`stats.hand`, the known lower bound), then the unseen cards as a muted bare `?` glyph + count when `handUnknown > 0`. After a 1px by 12px hairline divider, the dev cards whose type is not known as a muted `CounterGlyph devCard` + count: a rival's whole `devCards`, and for the claimed player whatever exceeds their typed hand. `No cards` in the faint colour when the cell would be empty. Rival hands no longer show ranges: the known count plus the `?` count carries the same facts.
- **Cards** column: `handTotal(stats)`, Inter 600 14.5px/20px, muted, `tabular-nums lining-nums`, centred. **VP** column: `standing.victoryPoints`, Inter 800 14.5px/20px, ink, same figures, centred. One font and size for both, never Georgia (its figures are old-style).
- **Your dev cards** (claimed player's row only): a wrapping line of pills under the main line, starting under the name text (spans all columns, left padding 18px = swatch plus gap), 5px gaps, 5px above. Order: playable kinds in `DEV_KINDS` order, then VP cards (`stats.vpCards`, star `CounterGlyph vpCard`, label `Victory point`), then cards bought this turn. A pill is 22px tall, fully round, padding `0 9px 0 8px`, hairline outline (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--line)`), no fill, 11.5px weight 600 ink, a 13px muted glyph then the label (`DEV_LABELS`). The count follows the label only when above one, as a bold digit (12px weight 800) in `--accent-dark`, 4px left margin, no ring or disc. A card bought this turn is a dashed pill (`border: 1px dashed var(--faint)`, no box-shadow, muted text) whose label is followed by `new` in bold `--accent-dark` (10.5px weight 800, 4px left margin) sitting on the label's baseline, titled `Bought this turn, playable next turn`. No pill for a kind held zero times; nothing at all when the claimed player has no typed cards and no VP cards.
- **Dev card glyphs**: one new `DevCardGlyph({ kind })` in `glyphs.tsx` on the 20-unit grid, filled `currentColor`, cut-outs in `PAPER`: Knight a sword, Road building two bars, Year of plenty a gift box, Monopoly a magnet (paths in the packet).
- **Unseen glyph**: `CounterGlyph`'s `unknownCard` becomes a bare `?` (2px round-capped stroke plus a dot, no card body) in `GLYPH_MUTED`. The Players tally uses the same shape.
- **Open row**: below the main line, first a read-only stats line (11.5px muted, bold ink numbers, 3px/14px gaps, indented to the name text): `<n> settlements`, `<n> city/cities` (cities plus super cities), `Longest road <n>`, `<n> knight(s) played`, `<n> dev cards`; the stat behind a held award is `--accent-dark`. Then today's stepper chips unchanged (`MoveStepper`: resources, Unseen, dev cards by type for the claimed player or Dev otherwise, Knights, VP), then a footer with muted `Each change is one undo step` on the left and a `Done` text button in `--accent-dark` on the right that closes the row. Each stepper tap is one commit, as today. The phone keeps the 30px by 44px stepper buttons (the existing `.best-moves-panel .move-chip button` rule).
- **Removed**: `BestMovesTable`, the Cards/Points switch, the Points view, the ranges, `Your dev cards`, the `Correct X by hand` box and their CSS (`.move-seg`, `.move-table`, `.move-who`, `.move-range`, `.move-mine`). `MoveStepper`, `ResourcePicker` and `CardAmounts` stay, in `BestMovesTable.tsx` renamed to `MoveInputs.tsx`. The note `Set your dev cards by type in the table` reads `... in the Ledger`.
- **Out of scope**: the Players popover's Pieces/Resources tally (`PlayerPanel`), which overlaps the Ledger; it gets only the new `?` glyph.
- **Follow-ups (owner 2026-11-12).** The desktop Players tally was dropped; the popover keeps the roster and award holders. Ledger row toggles keep `Correct <name>` and gain a screen-reader description referencing their player, hand, Cards and VP cells.
