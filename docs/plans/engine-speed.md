# Engine speed: every entry point in microseconds

Goal, set by Aki on 2026-10-12: bring the Catan engine into the range a chess engine works in, where one unit of work costs microseconds. Every row in the queue is done or dropped and on `main`. The work was done on branch `engine-speed`, worktree `.claude/worktrees/engine-speed`.

A policy decision already costs 0.02 to 2.5 µs. The entry points built on top of it are what this plan moves. Baseline is `main` at `4dab4d72`, `bench` on the laptop, minimum per call.

| Entry point | Baseline, 4 seats | Baseline, 6 seats | Target |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Adviser call, JSON in to JSON out | 155 to 170 µs | 220 to 260 µs | under 15 µs |
| Best picks in the browser (`analyzeBoard`, TypeScript) | about 1.4 s | 1.6 s, 6.3 s with three rival rankings | Rust in WebAssembly: at most 300 ms, and 1200 ms with three rival rankings |
| Whole self-play game | 2.2 ms | 6.4 ms | 10% and 15% faster, set in row 4 |

No item may change an output: `fixtures/advice-parity.json`, `fixtures/bench-hashes.json` and `fixtures/analysis-parity.json` stay byte-identical unless a row says otherwise.

## Tools kept outside the repo

`/Users/aki/code/unsettled/.claude/ralphex/best-picks-wasm/sweepGolden.test.ts` (git-excluded, beside the retired oracle) is the no-output-moved gate rows 7, 8 and 10 used: copy it to `src/engine/__tests__/`, run `SWEEP_GOLDEN=<file> npx vitest run src/engine/__tests__/sweepGolden.test.ts` once on the build to hold (it writes the hashes of the wasm's raw answers on 384 generated draft states and about 6,000 setup-road requests), then again after each rebuild (it compares). Time two wasm builds by alternating them in one Node run, and never trust a single timing: another project's jobs often hold most of the cores.
