# M-73 preregistration: the seat-count screen at 3 seats

Date: 2026-10-10. Committed before the pilot or any other run at this seat count.

**What this run asks.** Whether any of the twelve `sc_*` single-parameter arms beats today's vector at 3 seats on `standard4`, on seeds no parameter has seen. Every shipped weight was tuned at four seats, so this is a corner no placement weight has ever been read at. The rules it follows are fixed in [seat-count-programme.md](../../../.claude/specs/simulator/seat-count-programme.md) under "Procedure, fixed before any run" as amended by "Revision 1 to the procedure", and are applied here, not restated in full.

**Domain.** `tuning3`, seed `8796478869724336144` (`0x7a13_5eed_2026_1010`). It screens freely, so this run spends nothing. Board generation consumes layout and seat count, so these boards are not the boards any other seat count's screen drew. No invocation carries `tuning`, `eval`, `gate`, `tuning2`, `eval2` or `gate2`, all spent, and `eval3` and `gate3` are untouched by it.

**Design.** `evaluate`, `standard4`, 3 seats, field `app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json`, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial` with `--player-trading`, `--threads 0`, 2 reps, `--threshold 0.005`, `--alpha 0.05`, run from `simulator/`. Reference `base` is the field spec itself. Each of the twelve arms carries one `simulator/placement/arms/sc_*.json` on the hero path with the opponent path pinned to the field's weights. The arm values, each against the live default: `scarcityWeight` 0.175 and 0.7 (0.35), `coverageScarcityWeight` 0.25 and 1.0 (0.5), `coverageExponent` 1.0 and 2.25 (1.5), `diversityWeight` 0.8 and 3.2 (1.6), `portWeight` 0.275 and 1.1 (0.55), `expansionWeight` 0 and 0.3 (0.1).

**Commands.** The release binary is the one built with `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"` for M-67; it is rebuilt first, in its own command block, and must report `Finished` against a warm fingerprint. The pilot, from `simulator/`:

```text
target/release/unsettled-sim evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 3 --domain tuning3 --field app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm base=app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm scarcity_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_scarcity_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm scarcity_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_scarcity_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm covscarcity_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_covscarcity_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm covscarcity_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_covscarcity_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm coverage_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_coverage_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm coverage_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_coverage_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm diversity_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_diversity_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm diversity_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_diversity_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm port_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_port_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm port_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_port_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm expansion_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_expansion_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm expansion_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_expansion_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --reference base --boards 400 --reps 2 --policy heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --threads 0 --player-trading --threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --out runs/m73-seats3-pilot
```

The screen is that command with `--boards B` and `--out runs/m73-seats3-screen` and nothing else changed. The retry, if any arm earns it, is the screen's command with `--boards 4B`, `--out runs/m73-seats3-retry`, and the `--arm` list cut down to `base` plus the retried arms. A combine pass, if two or more axes survive, follows the programme file's "Candidate" rule on arm files committed before it runs.

**The pilot is read blind.** From `runs/m73-seats3-pilot` only the wall time, the illegal-action count, and per arm the selected interval's half-width and the discordant share are read. Its 400 boards are the first 400 of the screen's, so its estimates, signs, endpoints, win rates and verdicts are not read, recorded or cited.

**Sizing rule.** `B` is the smallest multiple of 500 at which the widest arm's pilot half-width times `sqrt(400 / B)` is at most 0.35pp, at least 500, and at most the largest multiple of 500 whose predicted wall time, the pilot's scaled by boards, stays under 30 minutes. At 3 seats a board is 6 paired units. If the cap binds, the screen runs at the cap and the entry records the half-widths that predicts.

**Disposition, per arm.** `better` survives. `worse` and `equivalent` drop the arm with that answer recorded. `inconclusive` with a point estimate of at least `+0.25pp` buys the single retry at `4B`; any other `inconclusive` drops the arm unresolved. On the retry `better` survives and anything else drops. No survivor means 3 seats keeps today's vector and neither `eval3` nor `gate3` is run at 3 seats. One or more survivors produce a candidate by the programme file's rule, and the confirmation on `eval3` gets its own preregistration.

**Admissibility and stop conditions.** `uptime` is recorded immediately before and after every invocation. No invocation shares a command block with a build, a test suite or another CPU-heavy job, none is piped through `tail` or `head`, and they run one after another. Every invocation must report domain `tuning3` at seed `8796478869724336144`. **Any illegal action, in the pilot, the screen or the retry, stops 3 seats.** `base`'s win rate on the screen and on the retry must sit within 3.29 binomial standard errors of `(1 - d) / 3`, `d` being its draw share; a miss stops 3 seats. `simulator/runs/` is gitignored, so no artifact enters the tree and the M entry is the record.

**Prediction (recorded before the pilot).** Three seats on `standard4` is the nearest neighbour of the corner the weights were tuned at: the same board, player trading on, six setup settlements instead of eight. Two seat counts have now been screened, and the same two axes moved at both: `coverage_lo` read `better` at four seats (M-67) and at two (M-70), and `diversity_hi` read `better` at two and positive but `inconclusive` at four. I expect three seats to sit between them. `coverage_lo` between `+0.3pp` and `+1.8pp`, the likeliest survivor; `coverage_hi` negative. `diversity_hi` between `+0.2pp` and `+1.4pp`, more likely than not above the retry line and about even to survive, since trading is on as at four seats, where it fell short; `diversity_lo` negative. `port_hi` clearly negative, between `-4.0pp` and `-1.0pp`, as at both screened seat counts; `port_lo` inside `+/-0.5pp`. `expansion_lo` negative, between `-1.0pp` and `+0.2pp`, and `expansion_hi` negative. The scarcity and coverage-scarcity low arms slightly positive and under the bar, their high arms negative. The corner is one third, so a board's units are noisier than at two seats and the time cap may bind. I put the chance of at least one survivor near 75%, most of it on `coverage_lo`.

**Scope, and what the entry may not be cited for.** One layout, one seat count, one policy, one domain, twelve single-parameter contrasts with no multiplicity correction. Each arm moves the hero's weights alone against 2 seats on today's vector, which was tuned at four seats and never at 3. The greedy opponent model inside every hero was measured in M-61 at four seats only, so no verdict may be described as taken against a correctly specified model. Both expansion arms move the setup road as well as the settlement. Nothing here transfers to another seat count.
