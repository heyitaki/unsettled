# M-68 preregistration: the 4-seat confirmation on `eval3`

Date: 2026-10-10. Committed before the run.

**Authorization, and the spend this run makes.** `eval3` is a held-out domain, spent at most once per seat count, and this run is that one spend at 4 seats. The user authorized it in advance on 2026-10-10, in the words [seat-count-programme.md](../../../.claude/specs/simulator/seat-count-programme.md) quotes under "Seed domains and the authorization": "Per seat count: screen on `tuning3`, confirm the survivor once on `eval3`, decide once on `gate3`. I pre-authorise those one-shot spends for each seat count." **`eval3` is spent at 4 seats by this run whatever it reads**, and after it no further `eval3` command may run on `standard4` at 4 seats. Board generation consumes layout and seat count, so the other seat counts' `eval3` boards are untouched.

**The condition that let this run be preregistered.** M-67 screened twelve arms at four seats on `tuning3` and left exactly one survivor. `coverage_lo` read `better` at `+1.083pp` on `[+0.766pp, +1.400pp]` over 36,000 paired units, and its mirror arm `coverage_hi` read `worse` at `-1.181pp`. `diversity_hi` was retried and read `inconclusive` twice, so it was dropped unresolved; every other arm was dropped at the screen. One survivor means no combine pass. Had the screen left no survivor this preregistration would not exist and `eval3` would stay unspent at four seats.

**Change under test.** One arm, `simulator/placement/arms/sc_coverage_lo.json`, against the field's own weights. It is the live `simulator/placement/default-weights.json` with `coverageExponent` moved from 1.5 to 1.0 and nothing else moved, pinned to exactly that leaf by `the_sc_arm_files_are_the_committed_seat_count_perturbations`, and 1.0 is the lower endpoint of that key's range in `simulator/placement/sweep-bounds.json`. At 1.0 coverage credit is linear in pips up to the cap, where at 1.5 a thinly produced resource earns less than its proportional share.

**Design.** `evaluate`, `standard4`, 4 seats, **`--domain eval3`**, field `app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json`, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial` with `--player-trading`, `--threads 0`, 18000 boards x 2 reps, which is 144,000 paired units over 18,000 clusters, `--threshold 0.005`, `--alpha 0.05`, run from `simulator/`. Reference `base` is the field spec itself. The decision arm `candidate` carries the candidate on the hero path with the opponent path pinned to the field's weights. The power is `4B`, four times the screen's boards, as the programme file fixed before any run.

**Command.** The release binary is rebuilt first with `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"` in its own command block and must report `Finished` against a warm fingerprint. Then the single invocation, from `simulator/`, with `uptime` recorded immediately before and after:

```text
target/release/unsettled-sim evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 4 --domain eval3 --field app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm base=app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm candidate=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_coverage_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --reference base --boards 18000 --reps 2 --policy heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --threads 0 --player-trading --threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --out runs/m68-seats4-eval3
```

**Decision rule (fixed before the run).** Verdict `better`, the whole selected interval above `+0.005`, confirms the candidate and sends it to the gate on `gate3`, which gets its own preregistration. **Any other outcome stops 4 seats on today's vector**, `worse`, `equivalent` or `inconclusive` regardless of point estimate, and `gate3` is then never run at 4 seats. There is no retry and no second `eval3` invocation at this seat count.

**Admissibility and stop conditions.** The invocation runs alone in its command block, unpiped. It must report domain `eval3` at seed `16691289016504815632`, the decimal form of `0xe7a3_5eed_2026_1010`. **Any illegal action stops 4 seats**, and so does a `base` win rate more than 3.29 binomial standard errors from `(1 - d) / 4`, `d` being its draw share; either way the domain is spent at this seat count and today's vector stays. `simulator/runs/` is gitignored, so the M entry is the record.

**Prediction (recorded before the run).** A point estimate between `+0.4pp` and `+1.3pp`, centred near `+0.85pp`. The screen's `+1.083pp` is the largest of twelve readings and was selected for being large, so I expect shrinkage of a few tenths, and the mirror arm reading the opposite sign at the same size is the main reason to believe most of it is real. The screen delivered a half-width of 0.317pp on this arm at 4500 boards, so I expect about `+/-0.16pp` here, which puts the `better` line near `+0.66pp`. I put `better` at about 75%, `inconclusive` at about 24%, and `equivalent` or `worse` together under 1%.

**Scope, and what the entry may not be cited for.** One contrast, one layout, one seat count, one policy, one domain, one spend. The arm moves the hero's weights alone against 3 seats on today's vector, so nothing here observes the world an adoption creates, where every seat at 4 seats carries the candidate. The greedy opponent model inside every hero was measured in M-61 at four seats only and found misspecified against a draft-aware field, so no verdict may be described as taken against a correctly specified model. The field is the same on `tuning3`, `eval3` and `gate3`, so a held-out domain controls for seeds and not for field-overfit.

**Numbering.** M-67 is the last entry in `.claude/specs/simulator/measurements.md` at this commit, so this run takes M-68.
