# M-67 preregistration: the seat-count screen at 4 seats

Date: 2026-10-10. Committed before the pilot or any other run.

**What this run asks.** Whether any of the twelve `sc_*` single-parameter arms beats today's vector at four seats on `standard4`, on seeds no parameter has seen. Four seats is the corner every shipped weight was tuned at, so the expected answer is no survivor, and the run goes first because it is the one seat count with priors to check the pipeline against. The rules it follows are fixed in [seat-count-programme.md](../../../.claude/specs/simulator/seat-count-programme.md) under "Procedure, fixed before any run" and are applied here, not restated in full.

**Domain.** `tuning3`, seed `8796478869724336144` (`0x7a13_5eed_2026_1010`), the third generation's screening domain. It screens freely, so this run spends nothing. No invocation carries `tuning`, `eval`, `gate`, `tuning2`, `eval2` or `gate2`, all spent, and `eval3` and `gate3` are untouched by it.

**Design.** `evaluate`, `standard4`, 4 seats, field `app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json`, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial` with `--player-trading`, `--threads 0`, 2 reps, `--threshold 0.005`, `--alpha 0.05`, run from `simulator/`. Reference `base` is the field spec itself. Each of the twelve arms carries one `simulator/placement/arms/sc_*.json` on the hero path with the opponent path pinned to the field's weights. The arm values, each against the live default: `scarcityWeight` 0.175 and 0.7 (0.35), `coverageScarcityWeight` 0.25 and 1.0 (0.5), `coverageExponent` 1.0 and 2.25 (1.5), `diversityWeight` 0.8 and 3.2 (1.6), `portWeight` 0.275 and 1.1 (0.55), `expansionWeight` 0 and 0.3 (0.1). `the_sc_arm_files_are_the_committed_seat_count_perturbations` in `simulator/crates/engine/src/policy/params_file.rs` pins each file to that one moved leaf.

**Commands.** Build first, from the repo root, in its own command block:

```text
RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings" cargo build --release --manifest-path simulator/Cargo.toml -p unsettled-sim
```

The pilot, from `simulator/`:

```text
target/release/unsettled-sim evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 4 --domain tuning3 --field app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm base=app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm scarcity_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_scarcity_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm scarcity_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_scarcity_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm covscarcity_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_covscarcity_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm covscarcity_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_covscarcity_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm coverage_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_coverage_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm coverage_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_coverage_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm diversity_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_diversity_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm diversity_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_diversity_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm port_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_port_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm port_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_port_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm expansion_lo=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_expansion_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm expansion_hi=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_expansion_hi.json@placement/default-weights.json --reference base --boards 400 --reps 2 --policy heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --threads 0 --player-trading --threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --out runs/m67-seats4-pilot
```

The screen is that command with `--boards B` and `--out runs/m67-seats4-screen` and nothing else changed. The retry, if any arm earns it, is the screen's command with `--boards 4B`, `--out runs/m67-seats4-retry`, and the `--arm` list cut down to `base` plus the retried arms. A combine pass, if two or more axes survive, follows the programme file's "Candidate" rule on arm files committed before it runs.

**The pilot is read blind.** From `runs/m67-seats4-pilot` only the wall time, the illegal-action count, and per arm the selected interval's half-width and the discordant share are read. Its 400 boards are the first 400 of the screen's, so its estimates, signs, endpoints, win rates and verdicts are not read, recorded or cited.

**Sizing rule.** `B` is the smallest multiple of 500 at which the widest arm's pilot half-width times `sqrt(400 / B)` is at most 0.35pp, at least 2000, and at most the largest multiple of 500 whose predicted wall time stays under 30 minutes. At four seats a board is 8 paired units. The closed programme's four-seat screens delivered about `+/-0.355pp` at 8000 boards on arms with a discordant share near 20%, so I expect `B` near 8000 unless an arm here moves far more picks than `expansionWeight` did.

**Disposition, per arm.** `better` survives. `worse` and `equivalent` drop the arm with that answer recorded. `inconclusive` with a point estimate of at least `+0.25pp` buys the single retry at `4B`; any other `inconclusive` drops the arm unresolved. On the retry `better` survives and anything else drops. No survivor means four seats keeps today's vector and neither `eval3` nor `gate3` is run at four seats. One or more survivors produce a candidate by the programme file's rule, and the confirmation on `eval3` gets its own preregistration.

**Admissibility and stop conditions.** The release binary is built with `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"` before any measurement and reports `Finished` against a warm fingerprint. `uptime` is recorded immediately before and after every invocation. No invocation shares a command block with a build, a test suite or another CPU-heavy job, none is piped through `tail` or `head`, and they run one after another. Every invocation must report domain `tuning3` at seed `8796478869724336144`. **Any illegal action, in the pilot, the screen or the retry, stops four seats.** `base`'s win rate on the screen and on the retry must sit within 3.29 binomial standard errors of `(1 - d) / 4`, `d` being its draw share; a miss stops four seats. `simulator/runs/` is gitignored, so no artifact enters the tree and the M entry is the record.

**Prediction (recorded before the pilot).** No survivor. `expansion_lo` removes the term M-66 adopted, so it should read near the negative of the readings that adopted it, between `-1.3pp` and `-0.5pp`, `worse` or `inconclusive` on the negative side; this is the calibration reading, and a positive estimate here would say something is wrong with the pipeline or that the adopted gain does not reproduce on a third seed generation. `expansion_hi` at 0.3 between `-0.9pp` and `0pp`, since M-62 read 0.3 well below 0.1 against a zero baseline. `diversity_hi` between `-0.2pp` and `+0.5pp`, most likely `equivalent`: M-62 read three times the weight at `+0.30pp` and this arm is twice. `diversity_lo` negative, between `-1.0pp` and `0pp`. The six scarcity and coverage arms and the two port arms I expect inside `+/-0.5pp`, mostly `equivalent`, on the argument that Phase H screened all of them at four seats on the older field and found no winner; `port_hi` and `coverage_lo` are the two I would least trust that argument on, because doubling the port weight and flattening the coverage curve both change which pair looks complete. I put the chance of at least one survivor near 10%.

**Scope, and what the entry may not be cited for.** One layout, one seat count, one policy, one domain, twelve single-parameter contrasts with no multiplicity correction. Each arm moves the hero's weights alone against three seats on today's vector. The readings are taken against the greedy opponent model M-61 measured at 39.4% of intervening picks named exactly, so no verdict may be described as taken against a correctly specified model. Both expansion arms move the setup road as well as the settlement. Nothing here transfers to another seat count; the other four have their own screens.

**Numbering.** M-66 is the last entry in `.claude/specs/simulator/measurements.md` at this commit, so this run takes M-67.
