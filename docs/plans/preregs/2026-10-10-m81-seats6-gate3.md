# M-81 preregistration: the 6-seat adoption gate on `gate3`

Date: 2026-10-10. Committed before the run.

**Authorization, and the spend this run makes.** `gate3` is a held-out domain, spent at most once per seat count, and this run is that one spend at 6 seats. The user authorized it in advance on 2026-10-10, in the words [seat-count-programme.md](../../../.claude/specs/simulator/seat-count-programme.md) quotes under "Seed domains and the authorization": "Per seat count: screen on `tuning3`, confirm the survivor once on `eval3`, decide once on `gate3`. I pre-authorise those one-shot spends for each seat count." **`gate3` is spent at 6 seats by this run whatever it reads**, and after it no further `gate3` command may run on `extension6` at 6 seats. Board generation consumes layout and seat count, so the other seat counts' `gate3` boards are untouched.

**The condition that let this run be preregistered.** M-80 read `better` on `eval3` at six seats: `+0.782pp` on a clustered interval of `[+0.625pp, +0.939pp]` over 120,000 paired units, the whole interval above the `+0.5pp` bar. That was the preregistered condition for running the gate; had it read anything else, this preregistration would not exist and `gate3` would stay unspent at six seats.

**Change under test.** One arm, `simulator/placement/arms/sc_coverage_lo.json`, against the field's own weights: the live `simulator/placement/default-weights.json` with `coverageExponent` moved from 1.5 to 1.0 and nothing else moved, pinned to exactly that leaf by `the_sc_arm_files_are_the_committed_seat_count_perturbations`. It is the vector M-79 screened and M-80 confirmed. An adoption here moves the six-seat entry alone: the 2, 3, 4 and 5 seat vectors and `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS` stay where they are.

**Design.** `evaluate`, `extension6`, 6 seats, **`--domain gate3`**, field `app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json`, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial` with `--player-trading`, `--threads 0`, 10000 boards x 2 reps, which is 120,000 paired units over 10,000 clusters, `--threshold 0.005`, `--alpha 0.05`, run from `simulator/`. Reference `base` is the field spec itself. The decision arm `candidate` carries the candidate on the hero path with the opponent path pinned to the field's weights. The power is `4B`, four times the screen's boards, as the programme file fixed before any run.

**Command.** The release binary is rebuilt first with `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"` in its own command block and must report `Finished` against a warm fingerprint. Then the single invocation, from `simulator/`, with `uptime` recorded immediately before and after:

```text
target/release/unsettled-sim evaluate --layout extension6 --seats 6 --domain gate3 --field app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm base=app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm candidate=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/sc_coverage_lo.json@placement/default-weights.json --reference base --boards 10000 --reps 2 --policy heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --threads 0 --player-trading --threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --out runs/m81-seats6-gate3
```

**Decision rule (fixed before the run).** Verdict `better`, the whole selected interval above `+0.005`, **adopts the candidate as the 6-seat vector**: its moved leaves go into `SEAT_WEIGHT_OVERRIDES[6]` in `src/engine/weights.ts`, `simulator/tools/generate-placement-parity.ts` regenerates `simulator/placement/seat-weights/seats-6.json`, and the `SEAT_ADOPTIONS` row in `params_file.rs` moves with it. **Any other outcome leaves 6 seats on today's vector**, `worse`, `equivalent` or `inconclusive` regardless of point estimate. There is no partial adoption, no retry and no second `gate3` invocation at this seat count. `DEFAULT_WEIGHTS` and `default-weights.json` do not move under either outcome.

**Admissibility and stop conditions.** The invocation runs alone in its command block, unpiped. It must report domain `gate3` at seed `7670578962881712144`, the decimal form of `0x6a73_5eed_2026_1010`. **Any illegal action stops 6 seats**, and so does a `base` win rate more than 3.29 binomial standard errors from `(1 - d) / 6`, `d` being its draw share; either way the domain is spent at this seat count and today's vector stays. `simulator/runs/` is gitignored, so the M entry is the record.

**Prediction (recorded before the run).** A point estimate between `+0.5pp` and `+1.1pp`, centred near `+0.8pp`. Two readings at this power agree, `+0.853pp` on the `tuning3` retry and `+0.782pp` on `eval3`, which was not selected on, so I centre near the second. M-80 delivered a half-width of 0.157pp, which puts the `better` line near `+0.66pp`, about a seventh of a point under my centre and less than two standard errors of this run's estimate from it. This is the gate most likely to miss of the five: I put `better` at about 88% and `inconclusive` at about 12%.

**Scope, and what the entry may not be cited for.** One contrast, one layout, one seat count, one policy, one domain, one spend. The arm moves the hero's weights alone against 5 seats on today's vector, so nothing here observes the world an adoption creates, where every seat at 6 seats carries the candidate. The greedy opponent model inside every hero was measured in M-61 at four seats only and found misspecified against a draft-aware field, so no verdict may be described as taken against a correctly specified model. The field is the same on `tuning3`, `eval3` and `gate3`, so a held-out domain controls for seeds and not for field-overfit.

**Numbering.** M-80 is the last entry in `.claude/specs/simulator/measurements.md` at this commit, so this run takes M-81.
