# M-36 preregistration — J5 frontier replacement at trial mixes

Date: 2026-08-25. Committed before the run.

**Change under test.** Phase J5 (SIM-GAP-28) replaces the degree-valued settlement expansion count with a measure of frontier actually opened, zero-default. `policy::frontier::opened` counts the distinct vertices a settlement at the candidate would newly open to the observer: adjacent unowned vertices the road network does not already reach, connected through an unowned edge (new road continuation points), plus the distance-rule-open sites one further unowned edge beyond them that the network does not already reach (newly settleable; girth six means nothing is double-counted). The blend is `degree + frontier_mix * (frontier - degree)` inside `vertex_score`'s settlement expansion closure, so it reaches every consumer of that term — build candidates, the goal chooser, and the road and pair expansion credits — through the one expression. A mix of zero never computes the frontier and keeps the degree count bit-for-bit (checked by the corpus recapture); one replaces it entirely, exactly (both counts are small integers). Trial mixes live behind the composite labels `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial-frontierlo` (0.5) and `-frontierhi` (1.0).

**Out of scope for this A/B.** The same task exposes `HeuristicParams::dev_buy_scale` (default 1.0, bit-identical), the SIM-GAP-24 seam: one overall scale on the development-card buy score, wrapping the deck-aware and legacy deck-blind spellings alike. It is mechanism only — no trial arm moves it here; the H2 policy-block sweep owns the answer.

**Known surface shape (recorded before the run).** Unlike the J1 need term, the frontier term's surface is live at most build-shaped decisions: on an empty interior fan the frontier (9) is three times the degree (3), and it collapses toward zero as the observer's own network already reaches the candidate's surroundings, so at mix 1.0 the expansion term swings by several times `expansion_weight` between frontier and interior candidates. Expect real discordance at both arms, larger at the full replacement.

**Design.** Standard paired A/B per the plan's measurement protocol: `evaluate`, standard4, 4 seats, `--domain tuning`, field `pip_diversity` placement, field policy `heuristic-v1-trader`, player trading on, 400 boards x 10 reps, threads 0. Reference = the full composite at mix zero; test arms = the composite at each trial mix.

**Command.**

```text
cargo run --release -p unsettled-sim -- evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 4 --domain tuning --field pip_diversity --arm base=pip_diversity --arm frontierlo=pip_diversity --arm frontierhi=pip_diversity --arm-policy base=heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --arm-policy frontierlo=heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial-frontierlo --arm-policy frontierhi=heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial-frontierhi --reference base --boards 400 --reps 10 --policy heuristic-v1-trader --threads 0 --player-trading --out runs/m36-frontier
```

**Decision rule (fixed before the run).** This is a screen of a zero-default seam, not a gap fix: the mix ships at zero whatever the verdicts, so no arm can change shipped behavior. A `better` verdict at a trial mix marks it as prior evidence for the H2 policy-block sweep, which owns the parameter; `equivalent`, `inconclusive`, or `worse` is recorded as-is and leaves it to H2. SIM-GAP-28 is deleted either way — the gap asks for the degree proxy to be replaced by a real frontier measure and measured, not for a win. SIM-GAP-24 is re-scoped to the H2 sweep of `dev_buy_scale`, not deleted, because no measurement here answers it. No adoption in either direction (Phase I is the user's).

**Threshold and alpha.** +-1pp practical threshold, interval selection as in prior M entries (McNemar vs clustered, whichever the harness selects).

**Admissibility.** `uptime` load recorded before and after; zero illegal actions required; single invocation (48,000 games across three paired arms) expected well under 5 minutes.
