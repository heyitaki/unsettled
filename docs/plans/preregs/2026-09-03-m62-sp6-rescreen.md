# M-62 preregistration: the SP6 re-screen against the draft-aware field

Date: 2026-09-03. Committed before the run.

**What this measures.** The four candidate axes SP6 carries, re-screened one at a time against the programme's new reference field. `expansionWeight` at 0.1 and 0.3, `expansionDecay` at 0.25 and 1.0 beside the surviving weight, `robberConcentrationWeight` at 4.0 and `diversityWeight` at 4.8. Each arm is the live `simulator/placement/default-weights.json` with one axis moved, carried on the hero path of the draft-aware kind while the opponent path stays pinned to the field's own weights, so an arm moves the hero's formula and nothing else. The product is a survivor set of `(key, value)` pairs, which Tasks 9 and 11 of `docs/plans/simulator-placement-sp6.md` read and which decides whether the combined vector exists at all.

**Why the numbers these axes already have cannot be reused.** The four axes were read on a field where every seat placed by `placement/mod.rs::choose_app_formula`, the greedy app-formula argmax over the shipped weights: `expansionWeight` in M-54, `robberConcentrationWeight` in M-57, `diversityWeight` in M-39 and M-40. The `## The reference field is the draft-aware kind, 2026-09-02` note in `.claude/specs/simulator/placement-programme.md` moved the field to `app_formula_draft:default-weights@default-weights`, all four seats, and the `## SP6 decisions, 2026-09-02` note above it says in terms that a field change re-opens the readings taken on the old field and that no SP3 through SP5 number may be cited as if taken on this one. Four separate mechanisms make that more than bookkeeping here, and each of them touches these axes specifically.

The first is the board an arm is scored on. Three rival seats now place by a lookahead rather than a single-pick argmax, so a hero candidate is evaluated against a different occupancy and, at the second pick, against a different set of surviving sites. `expansionWeight` is occupancy-dependent by construction: `simulator/crates/engine/src/placement/expansion.rs` walks outward over the current occupancy and a rival's road is impassable while a rival's vertex is a dead end. M-54's own scope paragraph says it is not a measurement of a field where every seat expands deliberately, and this is not that field either, but it is a differently occupied one, and the walk reads exactly the quantity that moved. `robberConcentrationWeight` is a property of the hero's own pair and is less exposed, and `diversityWeight` sits between them.

The second is that the hero is a different instrument. In M-54, M-57 and M-40 the arm was `app_formula:<arm file>`, a greedy argmax whose first pick maximized one settlement. Here it is `app_formula_draft:<arm file>@default-weights`, whose first pick already values the best second site surviving the intervening picks. That objective overlaps the expansion axis directly: a lookahead that prices the best surviving second settlement is pricing part of what the expansion walk was added to price, so the headroom the term had against a greedy hero may be partly gone. There is no way to bound that overlap from the existing entries, which is the whole reason for a re-screen rather than a transfer.

The third is that the reference moved by more than the arms did. M-59 measured the draft kind at `+1.6484pp` over the greedy kind on the same weights, the largest reading in the programme. Every one of these axes is now competing against a reference that already won that much, and a term worth a few tenths of a point against a weaker reference is not guaranteed to be worth the same against a stronger one.

The fourth is the estimator's own noise floor. `placement/mod.rs::choose_app_formula` breaks settlement and road ties from the picking seat's policy stream; `draft.rs` breaks both deterministically, settlement ties to the lowest vertex index and road ties to the lowest edge id, and draws nothing from the stream during setup. M-61 sized that tie rate at about 0.875% of picks on the old field. So every unit of divergence in this run is a divergence of substance, where the old readings carried some units that were coin flips on both sides.

**What this run does not fix, and may not be described away.** M-61 measured the greedy opponent model inside every hero at 39.4% of intervening picks named exactly against a draft-aware field, and 23.6% of first picks reaching the board the game actually reached. The model is therefore misspecified against the field it plays, and per the field-switch note no SP6 entry may call it correctly specified. What keeps that from biasing this contrast is that the misspecification is symmetric: every arm and the reference alike carry the same opponent weights file on the same greedy replay, so the arms differ from each other in the hero's formula and in nothing about the opponent model. The misspecification bounds what the readings mean, not whether they are comparable.

**The arms.** Six committed files under `simulator/placement/arms/`, each the live `default-weights.json` with only its declared keys moved, pinned by `simulator/crates/engine/src/policy/params_file.rs::the_sp6_arm_files_are_the_committed_rescreen_perturbations`, which also fails on a stray `sp6*` file nobody preregistered.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `exp_lo` | `sp6_expansion.json` | `expansionWeight` 0.1 | M-54, `+1.13pp` on `[+0.78pp, +1.48pp]`, unresolved at the old bar |
| `exp_hi` | `sp6_expansion_hi.json` | `expansionWeight` 0.3 | M-54, `+0.63pp` on `[+0.25pp, +1.01pp]`, unresolved at the old bar |

Every moved value sits inside its committed `sweep-bounds.json` range: `expansionWeight` 0.0 to 1.2, `expansionDecay` 0.125 to 1.0, `robberConcentrationWeight` 0.0 to 16.0, `diversityWeight` 0.4 to 6.4. Three of the six repeat a value an SP3 arm already carries and are minted rather than reused, because an arm file is a measurement record: `sp3_expansion_lo.json`, `sp3_expansion_hi.json` and `sp3_concentration.json` are cited by M-54 and M-57 as the vectors those entries ran, and Task 17 re-anchors each phase's pin to its own snapshot if adoption happens. `h1_diversity_48.json` could not have been reused on any reading: it is anchored to the pre-drop defaults at `handValueWeight` 0.4 and would move two keys.

**The decay pair moves two keys on purpose.** `expansionDecay` discounts a site by `expansionDecay ^ paidBuilds` inside a term the weight multiplies, so at `expansionWeight` 0 it multiplies nothing and a single-key decay arm would be byte-equivalent to the reference in behaviour. Both decay arms therefore carry `expansionWeight` 0.1, the value `sp6_expansion` carries, which is what makes their contrast against that reference a reading of the decay alone. `simulator/crates/engine/src/policy/params_file.rs::the_sp6_decay_arms_share_the_reference_arms_expansion_weight` pins that agreement so a later edit cannot quietly turn a decay contrast into a two-axis one.

**Two invocations, and the second is conditional.**

Invocation A screens the four non-decay arms against `base`, the field spec itself. Reference `base` = `app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json`, which is also the `--field` value, so `base` sits at the symmetric corner and its win rate is the drift check.

Invocation B screens the decay pair against `exp_ref` = `sp6_expansion.json` on the hero path, the same 0.1 the decay arms carry. **It runs only if the condition below holds, which is fixed now and read off invocation A's recorded verdicts without discretion.**

> Invocation B runs if and only if `exp_lo` reads `better` in invocation A, or `exp_lo` reads `inconclusive` and survives its preregistered retry as `better`. In either case the decay contrast is read against the value the decay arms carry, and the axis is being swept beside a weight that earned its place.
>
> If `exp_lo` does not survive, invocation B does not run, whatever `exp_hi` reads. Two sub-cases, and both give the same answer. If neither expansion arm survives, there is no weight to sweep a decay beside and the axis is second-order in a weight nobody is proposing to ship, which is the reasoning the `## SP3 decay axis closure, 2026-09-02` note already recorded for M-55. If `exp_hi` at 0.3 survives and `exp_lo` at 0.1 does not, the committed decay pair sits at 0.1 and would differ from an `exp_hi` reference on two axes at once, so the contrast would not be a decay reading and the plan's parenthetical naming `sp6_expansion_hi` as a possible reference cannot be honoured with these files. **No arm is minted after invocation A to rescue that branch**, because choosing an arm value after seeing a result is choosing the arm from the data, which is the rule M-57 held to when its own reading invited a larger value. The branch is recorded in the M entry, `expansionDecay` stays at its shipped 0.5 in every downstream vector, and the axis is recorded untested on this field rather than settled.

In every branch where invocation B does not run, Task 8 records why in the M entry, no `expansionDecay` value enters the survivor set, and the entry says the axis is untested rather than null.

**Design.** Both invocations are `evaluate`, `standard4`, 4 seats, `--domain tuning2`, 8000 boards x 2 reps, which is 64,000 paired units per arm over 8,000 clusters, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial` with `--player-trading`, `--threads 0`, **`--threshold 0.005`**, `--alpha 0.05`. That is SP6's screen power at the 0.5 bar, four times the boards SP3 through SP5 screened at, which is what resolving at the tighter bar costs and is the arithmetic behind the threshold decision in the SP6 decisions note. `tuning2` is SP6's screening domain, minted by Task 2 with seed `8796197394747623682`; `tuning`, `eval` and `gate` are spent and no invocation here carries any of them, and `eval2` and `gate2` are untouched by this run.

**Power.** The bar is 0.5pp and the four priors sit between `+0.58pp` and `+1.3pp`, so this run is sized to separate a real reading of that size from the bar rather than from zero. M-54's retry at this exact power delivered half-widths of `+/-0.35pp` and `+/-0.38pp` on the two expansion arms, M-59's retry `+/-0.34pp` and M-60's `+/-0.17pp` and `+/-0.23pp`. A half-width near `+/-0.35pp` around a point estimate of `+1.1pp` clears `+0.5pp` comfortably; one around `+0.58pp` does not, and that is the honest reason `conc` is the arm most likely to come back `inconclusive`. The retry the disposition rule buys, at 32000 boards x 2 reps, would halve those half-widths again.

**Timing, and why a pilot is registered for a run that is not over 32,000 boards.** Every game in this run puts a lookahead on all four seats, which no `evaluate` in the programme has done: M-59 and M-60 ran one draft seat against three greedy ones. M-61's `diagnose` is the closest measured point, 4,000 four-seat draft games plus the accuracy statistic in 1.452s at 18 workers, about 2,755 games/s. Invocation A plays 5 arms x 8000 boards x 2 reps x 4 hero rotations, **320,000 games**, which extrapolates to about 116 seconds. Two of its five arms carry a nonzero `expansionWeight` on the hero path, and that is the specific reason for the pilot: `draft.rs::Lookahead::new` turns the `ScoreRows` cache off whenever either scorer carries a nonzero `expansionWeight`, so those two arms recompute a score row per replay state instead of once per `(seat, holdings, grant)` triple, on top of running the outward walk on every candidate. Nothing in the record bounds that cost, so the pilot at 200 boards runs first and its wall time is multiplied by 40. **If the extrapolation puts invocation A over eight minutes it is launched in the background and polled with a loop that prints the elapsed minute**, per the plan's long-run rule; otherwise it runs in the foreground. The pilot's own artifact is a wall-clock reading and nothing else: **its estimates and verdicts are not read, not recorded and not cited**, in this entry or anywhere, because a 200-board look at the same domain before the real run is exactly the peeking the preregistration discipline exists to prevent. Invocation B plays 3 arms, 192,000 games, all three carrying a nonzero hero `expansionWeight`, so it is extrapolated from invocation A's measured per-game cost rather than from the pilot and the same eight-minute rule applies to it.

**Decision rule (fixed before the run).** The plan's standing disposition rule at the 0.5 bar, applied per arm without discretion. `better`, the whole selected interval above `+0.005`, records that arm's `(key, value)` pair as a survivor. `worse` drops it and the entry records why the axis was expected to help. `equivalent` drops it and records the null. `inconclusive` buys units exactly once, at four times the boards (`--boards 32000`, `--reps` unchanged at 2) in a single retry invocation carrying every `inconclusive` arm, and an arm still `inconclusive` after that retry is recorded unresolved and dropped from the package.

**The expansion axis contributes at most one value**, because `exp_lo` and `exp_hi` are two points on one axis and a combined vector holds one number per key. If both read `better`, the survivor is the larger point estimate; on a tie to the last recorded digit it is the smaller weight, on the standing preference for the more conservative value. The decay axis contributes at most one value on the same rule, read from invocation B alone. `robberConcentrationWeight` and `diversityWeight` each stand or fall on their single arm. No multiplicity correction is applied across the four arms of invocation A and none is claimed: each is read against the shared reference on its own, and the disposition rule is per-axis rather than a family hypothesis, which is the same position M-54's preregistration took.

**The survivor set is recorded explicitly as `(key, value)` pairs in the last paragraph of the M entry**, because Task 9 branches on its size: fewer than two survivors skips the coordinate pass, and none skips Tasks 10 through 17 entirely. An empty survivor set is a legitimate outcome of this run and is recorded as one rather than argued around.

**Record only, in every branch.** No shipped default moves: not `src/engine/weights.ts::DEFAULT_WEIGHTS`, not `simulator/placement/default-weights.json`, not `simulator/placement/phase-i-candidate-weights.json`, not any params file. The six arm files stay the only committed `sp6*` files carrying a nonzero value on any of these axes. `SIM-GAP-34` stays open in `.claude/specs/simulator/gaps.md` whatever this run reads, because that gap closes on adoption and adoption is Task 17's business behind two further verdicts.

**Prediction (recorded before the run).** Stated per arm, with the reasoning, so the entry can be scored against it.

`exp_lo` at 0.1: I predict a point estimate between `+0.3pp` and `+1.0pp`, below M-54's `+1.13pp`, most likely `better` and otherwise `inconclusive`. The direction is not in doubt, since M-54 settled the sign on 64,000 units at this power, and the reason to expect shrinkage is the second mechanism above: the draft kind's first pick already values the best surviving second site, so a term that pays for what a candidate opens is partly redundant with the objective it now rides on. If the shrinkage is small the arm clears the bar easily; if the redundancy is most of the term the arm lands near the bar and reads `inconclusive`.

`exp_hi` at 0.3: between `0pp` and `+0.6pp`, most likely `equivalent` or `inconclusive`. M-54 established that the larger weight changes more picks and converts fewer of them, and the redundancy argument bites harder at a weight with more authority to override the production ranking. I expect `exp_lo` to beat `exp_hi` again, and I would be surprised to see the ordering reverse.

`conc` at 4.0: between `+0.2pp` and `+0.8pp`, most likely `inconclusive`, with `better` and `equivalent` both live. This is the arm the bar was moved for: M-57 read `+0.575pp` on `[+0.23pp, +0.92pp]`, which is `equivalent` at 1pp and `better` at 0.5pp only if the new interval's lower edge clears `+0.5pp`, and M-57's lower edge was `+0.23pp`. Four times the boards halves the half-width to about `+/-0.18pp`, so an estimate holding near `+0.58pp` would read `better` by a small margin and an estimate slipping to `+0.45pp` would read `equivalent`. The axis is a property of the hero's own pair and is the least exposed of the four to the field change, so I expect the smallest shift from its old reading.

`div` at 4.8: between `+0.6pp` and `+1.5pp`, most likely `better`. This is the strongest prior in the set, `+1.3pp` on `[+0.95pp, +1.63pp]` at exactly this power, and it is the only one of the four whose old interval already sat entirely above the new bar. It is also the axis furthest from the lookahead's own objective, since a pair's resource spread is priced the same way whoever picks next, so the redundancy argument that drives the expansion predictions does not apply to it. The mechanism that could still move it is the first one, a differently occupied board changing which pairs are reachable.

`decay_lo` at 0.25 and `decay_hi` at 1.0, if they run: both between `-0.4pp` and `+0.4pp`, most likely `equivalent`, and I expect `decay_lo` above `decay_hi`. A shallower discount concentrates the term on the nearest site and a decay of 1.0 pays the second ring at full value regardless of distance, and the term already takes only the best two sites, so the shipped 0.5 is not obviously mis-set in either direction. The honest position is that this axis has no prior at all and the prediction is arithmetic about the discount rather than evidence.

Across the set I predict **two or three survivors**, most likely `div` alone or `div` with one of `exp_lo` and `conc`. Two readings would change how the rest of SP6 is described and are named now so they cannot be explained away later. **Every arm reading `worse`** would say the field change did not merely shrink these effects but reversed them, which would mean the axes were pricing something the greedy field left on the table and the lookahead now takes, and SP6 would close on an empty survivor set at Task 9's skip branch. **`exp_hi` clearly above `exp_lo`** would contradict M-54's clearest secondary finding and would say the weight's right scale depends on the field it is measured against, which no entry in the programme currently allows for.

**Threshold and alpha.** `+/-0.5pp` practical threshold, passed as `--threshold 0.005` because the flag takes a fraction, per SP6's standing threshold in `.claude/specs/simulator/contracts.md`. Alpha 0.05. Interval selection is the harness's own rule in `simulator/crates/cli/src/evaluate.rs::try_paired_stats`, the wider of the McNemar and board-clustered intervals.

**Admissibility.** The release binary is built with `RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings"` before any measurement starts and is confirmed to report `Finished` against a warm fingerprint, which is M-50's lesson. `uptime` is recorded immediately before and immediately after every invocation and every reading enters the M entry, the pilot included. No invocation shares its command block with a build, a test suite or any other CPU-heavy job, and the invocations run one after another rather than in parallel. **Zero illegal actions required in every invocation**, or the run is inadmissible. Every invocation must report domain `tuning2` at seed `8796197394747623682`; a different domain or seed makes it inadmissible. Invocation A's `base` win rate is checked against the symmetric 0.25 corner, as every screen since M-51 has checked its reference, and because `base` carries the field spec exactly this is also the drift check: an unintended touch of the shipped path shows there first. **Invocation A's `base` is not required to reproduce any earlier entry's figure and must not**, because both the field and the domain moved: M-54's, M-57's, M-58's, M-59's and M-60's references ran the greedy kind on `tuning`, and M-61 is a diagnostic with no reference at all. The first four-seat draft-aware `base` win rate on `tuning2` is established by this run, and later SP6 entries reproduce it rather than the other way round. Invocation B's reference is `exp_ref` rather than `base`, so it sits at no symmetric corner and no corner check applies to it; what is checked instead is that `exp_ref`'s win rate matches invocation A's `exp_lo` win rate to the game, since the two are the same spec on the same domain and board schedule, and a mismatch means the schedule moved. `simulator/runs/` is gitignored, so no artifact enters the tree and the M entry is the record.

**Scope, and what the entry may not be cited for.** Six contrasts across four axes, one layout, one seat count, one policy, one domain. Every arm moves the hero's weights alone against three seats carrying the field's, so nothing here measures a field where every seat carries the arm's value, and no reading transfers to `extension6` or to 3, 5 and 6 seat games. The two expansion arms and both decay arms move the setup road as well as the settlement, because `draft.rs::setup_road` applies SP3's road rule and falls through to the far-endpoint scoring only at `expansionWeight` 0, so their readings bundle the two effects and this run does not separate them; the same is true of `conc` and `div` only in that they leave the road rule alone, which makes them the cleaner contrasts of the six. The readings are taken against an opponent model M-61 measured at 39.4% pick accuracy against this field, so no verdict here may be described as taken against a correctly specified model. A single arm's verdict says what that axis is worth alone and says nothing about what it is worth beside another, which is the question Task 9's combined vector and coordinate pass exist to ask and the failure M-44 recorded when it was assumed instead.

**Numbering.** M-61 is the last entry in `.claude/specs/simulator/measurements.md` at this commit, so this run takes M-62. If that file has moved on by the time the run executes, the entry takes the next unused number and this preregistration is cited by filename.
