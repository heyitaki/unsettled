# M-100 preregistration: the 4-seat screen of the cut-off arms on `tuning4`

Date: 2026-10-13. Committed before the invocation executes.

**Why this run exists.** Item 4 of [cut-off-programme.md](../../../.claude/specs/simulator/cut-off-programme.md): the first reading of whether a contest discount or the first-pick re-pricing wins games at 4 seats. M-95 kept all four arms (each changes at least 2% of drafts) and fixed the size from a pilot whose outcomes were not read.

**Spend.** `tuning4` is this programme's screening domain and is already spent at 4 seats by the pilot. `eval4` and `gate5` are not touched here.

**Arms.** `co_flat_25_s4.json`, `co_flat_50_s4.json`, `co_flat_75_s4.json` (`contestWeight` 0.25, 0.5, 0.75) and `co_reprice_only_s4.json` (`repriceFirstExpansion` 1), each the 4-seat vector as it stood before this programme with one leaf moved on the hero's path, the opponent path pinned to the field's weights.

**Design.** `evaluate`, `standard4`, 4 seats, `--domain tuning4`, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial`, `--player-trading`, field and reference `base` both the 4-seat vector, **5,500 boards** x 2 reps, the `B` that M-95 fixed (predicted half-width 0.00339 for the widest arm), `--threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --threads 0`, run from `simulator/` alone on the machine's simulator workload with `uptime` recorded before and after.

**Command.**

```text
target/release/unsettled-sim evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 4 --domain tuning4 --field 'app_formula_draft:placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json@placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json' --arm 'base=app_formula_draft:placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json@placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json' --arm 'flat_25=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/co_flat_25_s{seats}.json@placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json' --arm 'flat_50=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/co_flat_50_s{seats}.json@placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json' --arm 'flat_75=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/co_flat_75_s{seats}.json@placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json' --arm 'reprice_only=app_formula_draft:placement/arms/co_reprice_only_s{seats}.json@placement/seat-weights/seats-{seats}.json' --reference base --boards 5500 --reps 2 --policy heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --threads 0 --player-trading --threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --out runs/m100-seats4-screen
```

**Decision rule (requirement 29, fixed before the run).** `pair_verdict` reads each arm. `better` survives. `worse` or `equivalent` is dropped with that answer recorded. `inconclusive` with `estimate` at or above 0.0025 buys one retry, `base` and the retried arms at 22,000 boards under its own preregistration; any other `inconclusive` arm is dropped unresolved. An arm's last reading is its retry if it had one and its screen otherwise.

**What follows from the last readings (requirements 30 and 31).** If some flat arm and `reprice_only` each have a positive estimate at their last reading and neither read `worse`, one second-stage arm `flat_reprice` is created, screened and retried by the same rule: the 4-seat vector with the `contestWeight` of the flat arm whose last-reading estimate is highest and `repriceFirstExpansion` 1. The seat count's candidate is the arm with the highest last-reading estimate among those that read `better`; ties go to the arm listed first in the order `flat_25`, `flat_50`, `flat_75`, `reprice_only`, `flat_reprice`. With no `better` arm, 4 seats closes with both keys at 0, and `eval4` and `gate5` stay unspent there.

**Stop conditions.** `illegalActions` above 0, or a `base` arm that misses the symmetric corner: with `c = (1 - draws / games) / 4`, a miss is `abs(wins / games - c) > 3.29 * sqrt(c * (1 - c) / games)`. Either stops 4 seats at this run with both keys at 0, and the entry records why.

**Prediction (recorded before the run).** The flat arms change between a fifth and a half of drafts, so an effect of either sign has room to show, but the discount is a blunt rule and the vet scored this programme 5 of 10. I expect every estimate inside plus or minus 0.5pp and most arms to read `equivalent` or `inconclusive`. I put some arm reading `better` at about 15%, some flat arm reading `worse` at about 25%, and a retry being bought at about 20%.

**Scope.** One layout, one seat count, one policy, one field, the hero's formula alone. Nothing read here changes a command at another seat count.

**Numbering.** The five screens take M-100 to M-104 in the order 4, 2, 3, 5, 6, so this one is M-100. Retries and second-stage runs take the next free numbers as they are preregistered.
