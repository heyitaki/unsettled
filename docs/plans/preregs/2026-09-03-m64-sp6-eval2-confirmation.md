# M-64 preregistration: the SP6 confirmation on `eval2`

Date: 2026-09-03. Committed before the run.

**Authorization, and the spend this run makes.** `eval2` is a held-out domain reserved for exactly one SP6 reading, and this run is that reading. The `## SP6 decisions, 2026-09-02` note in `.claude/specs/simulator/placement-programme.md` records the authorization in terms: on 2026-09-02, in advance of either run, the user authorized this run to spend `eval2` exactly once on the SP6 confirmation and `gate2` exactly once on the SP6 adoption decision, and no further approval is sought at the point of spending. **`eval2` is spent by this run for these parameters, and it is not re-run whatever it reads.** No retry is preregistered and none may be bought: the standing disposition rule's `inconclusive` retry is a screening rule for `tuning2` and does not reach a held-out domain, and Task 11 of `docs/plans/simulator-placement-sp6.md` fixes this run as a single invocation whose verdict stands. After this run no further `eval2` command may ever run, on these parameters or any others.

**Change under test.** One arm, the SP6 candidate vector, against the field's own weights. The candidate is `simulator/placement/sp6-candidate-weights.json`, which M-63 recorded as the output of a coordinate pass that was not needed: M-62's re-screen of the four SP6 candidate axes against the draft-aware field left exactly one survivor, `(expansionWeight, 0.1)`, so the combined vector is the live `simulator/placement/default-weights.json` with `expansionWeight` moved from 0 to 0.1 and nothing else moved. `simulator/crates/engine/src/policy/params_file.rs::the_sp6_candidate_file_records_the_combined_vector` walks every leaf of the shipped vector and pins that the moved set is exactly that one axis at that value, checks the value against `simulator/placement/sweep-bounds.json`, where `expansionWeight` runs 0.0 to 1.2, and pins the file equal to `simulator/placement/arms/sp6_expansion.json`. The three dropped axes keep their shipped values in the candidate: `expansionDecay` at 0.5, `robberConcentrationWeight` at 0, `diversityWeight` at 1.6.

**What this run asks, and what it does not.** The question is whether the tuning-domain gain the candidate earned is a property of the parameter or of the `tuning2` seeds. M-62 read `sp6_expansion.json` against `base` twice on `tuning2`, `+0.889pp` on `[+0.539pp, +1.240pp]` at 64,000 paired units and `+0.845pp` on `[+0.668pp, +1.022pp]` at 256,000, both `better`, so the sign and rough size are settled on that domain and this run is not asking about them again. What a domain change controls is seed luck alone. It does not control field-overfit, because the field is the same on `eval2` as on `tuning2`, and it does not control the opponent model: M-61 measured the greedy model inside every hero at 39.4% of intervening picks named exactly against a draft-aware field, so per the `## The reference field is the draft-aware kind, 2026-09-02` note no verdict here may be described as taken against a correctly specified model. Field-overfit is the gate's residual risk in M-46's sense and stays priced rather than removed.

**Why this is a single-arm run.** One question, one decision arm, no multiplicity, following M-45's confirmation-at-power protocol and M-46's gate. The three axes M-62 dropped get no confirmation arm: `expansionDecay` and `diversityWeight` were resolved `equivalent` and `robberConcentrationWeight` was recorded unresolved after `inconclusive` at both powers, and re-asking any of them on a held-out domain would spend part of a spend-once reading on a question the screening domain already answered. The field switch is not on trial here either. It rests on M-59 and M-61 and landed independently of the weight package, so a rejection at this run leaves the draft-aware kind the reference field and moves only the candidate vector.

**Design.** `evaluate`, `standard4`, 4 seats, **`--domain eval2`**, field `app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json`, every seat on `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial` with `--player-trading`, `--threads 0`, 8000 boards x 2 reps, which is 64,000 paired units over 8,000 clusters, **`--threshold 0.005`**, `--alpha 0.05`, run from `simulator/`. Reference `base` is the field spec itself, so it sits at the symmetric corner and its win rate is the drift check. The decision arm `candidate` carries `sp6-candidate-weights.json` on the hero path with the opponent path pinned to the field's own weights, per the draft-aware arm contract in `.claude/specs/simulator/contracts.md`, so the arm moves the hero's formula and nothing about the opponent model.

**Commands.** Build first, from the repo root, so no compile lands inside a timed window:

```text
RUSTFLAGS="-D warnings" cargo build --release --manifest-path simulator/Cargo.toml -p unsettled-sim
```

Then the single invocation, from `simulator/`, with `uptime` recorded immediately before and immediately after:

```text
target/release/unsettled-sim evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 4 --domain eval2 --field app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm base=app_formula_draft:placement/default-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --arm candidate=app_formula_draft:placement/sp6-candidate-weights.json@placement/default-weights.json --reference base --boards 8000 --reps 2 --policy heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --threads 0 --player-trading --threshold 0.005 --alpha 0.05 --out runs/m64-sp6-eval2
```

The two weights files in this run have distinct stems, `default-weights` and `sp6-candidate-weights`, which is what keeps `heuristics.rs::parse_heuristic` from rejecting the pair on a label collision. The invocation is not piped through `tail` or `head` and does not share its command block with the build, a test suite or any other CPU-heavy job.

**Timing, and why no pilot is registered.** This run plays 2 arms x 8000 boards x 2 reps x 4 hero rotations, **128,000 games**. M-62 measured the two per-game costs this run needs, as arithmetic on its two wall times at 18 workers: an arm carrying a nonzero `expansionWeight` costs about 6.73e-4s per game because `draft.rs::Lookahead::new` turns the `ScoreRows` cache off, and a zero-`expansionWeight` arm about 2.21e-4s. `base` is a zero arm and `candidate` a nonzero one, 64,000 games each, so the run extrapolates to about **57 seconds**. That is far under the eight-minute bar, so it runs in the foreground, and it carries no new layout and fewer than 32,000 boards, so the plan's pilot rule does not fire.

**Decision rule (fixed before the run).** Verdict `better`, the whole selected interval above `+0.005`, confirms the package and **the gate runs**: Task 15 preregisters M-66 on `gate2` and Task 16 executes it. **Any other outcome rejects the package**: `worse`, `equivalent`, or `inconclusive` regardless of point estimate. On a rejection, Tasks 15 through 17 take their skip branch, a dated rejection note is appended to `.claude/specs/simulator/placement-programme.md` saying the package is rejected and nothing moved, and **`gate2` is left unspent for a future programme**. There is no partial adoption, no second `eval2` invocation and no retry under any outcome.

**Power, and the one risk this design carries.** This is the run's chief hazard and it is named here so it cannot be explained away afterwards. The power is 8000 boards x 2 reps, fixed by Task 11 of the plan, which is the same 64,000 paired units M-62's screen used. M-62's screen delivered a half-width of `+/-0.35pp` on this exact contrast. A `better` verdict at the 0.5 bar needs the interval's lower edge above `+0.5pp`, so with a half-width near `+/-0.35pp` it needs a point estimate above about `+0.85pp`. M-62's two estimates for this contrast are `+0.889pp` and `+0.845pp`, which straddle that figure. **The run is therefore close to a coin flip by construction even if the effect reproduces exactly**, and an `inconclusive` reading here would reject a package whose sign and rough size are settled on 320,000 `tuning2` units rather than say the effect is absent. The retry that would resolve it, at four times the boards, is not available: `eval2` is spend-once and the decision rule forbids a second invocation. I record now that a rejection on `inconclusive` should be read in the record as a power outcome and not as a null, and that the 32000-board reading M-62 already holds on `tuning2` is the tighter estimate of the effect's size, though on the screening domain rather than a held-out one.

**Prediction (recorded before the run).** I predict a point estimate between `+0.5pp` and `+1.1pp`, centred near `+0.85pp`, because the seed change is the only move and M-62's two `tuning2` readings agree with each other to within 0.04pp across a fourfold change in units. On the verdict I predict `better` and `inconclusive` at close to even odds, slightly favouring `inconclusive`, for the arithmetic in the paragraph above: the `better` threshold at this power sits at about `+0.85pp` and the best estimate of the effect sits at `+0.845pp`. `equivalent` is live but less likely, needing the estimate to fall to about `+0.15pp` for the upper edge to come under the bar. `worse` would be a genuine surprise and would say the `tuning2` gain was seed-specific in direction and not merely in size, which two readings at two powers on 320,000 units make hard to credit. On the slot structure I predict the mild slope M-62's retry found, roughly `+1.0`, `+1.2`, `+0.6`, `+0.5` across slots 0 to 3, rather than M-54's slot-0 spike, which was a property of the greedy field.
