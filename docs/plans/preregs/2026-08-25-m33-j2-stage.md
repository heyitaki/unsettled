# M-33 preregistration — J2 stage signal at trial values

Date: 2026-08-25. Committed before the run.

**Change under test.** Phase J2 builds the stage signal (`policy::stage::Stage`), derived once per decision: `lateness = 1 - min(piece_frac, site_frac)`, where `piece_frac` is the observer's remaining settlement+city pieces over the rule set's 5+4 supply and `site_frac` is the count of distance-rule-open settlement sites board-wide over the settlement supply, capped at 1 (the site channel binds only once open sites drop below the supply). Under gated policies the denial context's top rival danger — the shared ETW danger model — raises the lateness by `stage_urgency_weight * danger`, clamped to 1; ungated paths carry no belief and read the piece/site channels alone. Two consumers in `vertex_score_with`, both zero-default: the settlement expansion term is scaled by `(1 - stage_expansion_weight * lateness).max(0)`, and the city vertex score is scaled by `1 + stage_city_weight * lateness`. The stage is pure state (no fixed point), so unlike the J1 need term it also reaches the goal chooser and the road/pair expansion credits. All three weights default to zero, which never derives the stage and keeps every shipped policy's scores bit-identical (checked by the corpus diff); the trial triples live behind the composite labels `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial-stagelo` (expansion 0.5, city 0.5, urgency 0.5) and `-stagehi` (expansion 1.0, city 2.0, urgency 1.0).

**Design.** Standard paired A/B per the plan's measurement protocol: `evaluate`, standard4, 4 seats, `--domain tuning`, field `pip_diversity` placement, field policy `heuristic-v1-trader`, player trading on, 400 boards x 10 reps, threads 0. Reference = the full composite at all-zero stage weights; test arms = the composite at each trial triple.

**Command.**

```text
cargo run --release -p unsettled-sim -- evaluate --layout standard4 --seats 4 --domain tuning --field pip_diversity --arm base=pip_diversity --arm stagelo=pip_diversity --arm stagehi=pip_diversity --arm-policy base=heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial --arm-policy stagelo=heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial-stagelo --arm-policy stagehi=heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial-stagehi --reference base --boards 400 --reps 10 --policy heuristic-v1-trader --threads 0 --player-trading --out runs/m33-stage
```

**Decision rule (fixed before the run).** This is a screen of a zero-default seam, not a gap fix: the weights ship at zero whatever the verdicts, so no arm can change shipped behavior. A `better` verdict at a trial triple marks it as prior evidence for the H2 policy-block sweep, which owns the three parameters; `equivalent`, `inconclusive`, or `worse` is recorded as-is and leaves them to H2 with no prior. No adoption in either direction (Phase I is the user's).

**Threshold and alpha.** +-1pp practical threshold, interval selection as in prior M entries (McNemar vs clustered, whichever the harness selects).

**Admissibility.** `uptime` load recorded before and after; zero illegal actions required; single invocation (48,000 games across three paired arms) expected well under 5 minutes.
