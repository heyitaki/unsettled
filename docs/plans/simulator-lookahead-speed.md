# Simulator lookahead speed

Approved 2026-10-10, steps revised 2026-10-11. Goal: remove the cost the `expansionWeight` adoption (M-66) added to the draft-aware setup lookahead, with every pick and every game outcome byte-identical.

## Why

A single-threaded profile of `simulate` on the main-tree release binary (four seats, `app_formula_draft` on every seat with the shipped weights, policy `heuristic-v1-trader-aware-threat-devcards-denial`, `--seed 7`, no seed domain) put 97% of worker time in `GameArena::setup_pick`: `expansion::term` self time 37%, libm `pow` 35%, diversity and port scoring 21%. Playing the game was 3% and `DecisionView` 0.4%. The profile was taken under load on one fixed board, so the shares are indicative.

Cause: `ScoreRows` keeps a seat's score row only while both formulas carry `expansionWeight` 0 (`draft.rs`, the `reusable` flag in `Lookahead::new`). Since the adoption every replayed pick rescores every vertex, each rescore prices every expansion site the vertex opens, and each site price runs `diversity_delta`, which calls `coverage` (a `powf`) ten times.

## Constraints

- Byte-identical outputs. The scorer mirrors `src/engine/` and the `W13` parity class pins the two, so no `powf` call may be replaced by `powi`, `sqrt` or a multiplication, and no float sum may change its order. A hoisted or memoised value must be the value the same call would have produced.
- A site's price (`total_with_holdings(holdings + candidate, site, false)`) depends on the formula, the holdings, the candidate and the site, never on occupancy. The walk that finds the sites does depend on occupancy and is not cached.
- The setup hot loop stays at zero steady-state allocations (`cli/tests/alloc.rs`).
- No seed domain is spent: `bench` runs on `PERF_SEED` and `simulate` on a plain `--seed`.
- Rows that exercise the lookahead: `placement/{standard4,extension6}/draft/first`, `draft/middle`, `full_draft`, and the `e2e` rows. The `greedy`, `score_all` and `prepare` rows are controls and should not move, except `score_all` and `greedy` through the hoist in `score_row`.

## Replaced steps

The first version of this plan captured its own byte-exact corpus and wall-clock timings. The bench suite's pinned decision hashes and box instruction counts cover both, and do not depend on machine load, so those steps were dropped.
